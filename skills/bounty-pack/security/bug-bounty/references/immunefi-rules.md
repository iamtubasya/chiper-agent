# Immunefi Platform Rules — CRITICAL

## Whitehat Prohibited Behaviors (Violation = PERMANENT BAN)

### 1. Testing Restrictions
- ❌ **Testing on mainnet or public testnet** → PERMANENT BAN
- ❌ Misrepresenting assets/severity/impacts in scope
- ❌ Automated testing generating significant traffic
- ❌ Exploiting/attacking or threatening to exploit/attack a project
- ❌ Whitehacking to save funds without project's express written consent

### 2. Communication Restrictions
- ❌ Phishing or social engineering against Immunefi/projects
- ❌ Contacting non-support staff at Immunefi
- ❌ Routing around Immunefi to communicate directly with project
- ❌ Submitting via email or other channels (ONLY Immunefi platform)
- ❌ Harassment, abusive/bad faith communication

### 3. Submission Restrictions
- ❌ Beg bounty behavior
- ❌ Placeholder bug submissions (vague title, few details, no repro steps)
- ❌ Submitting bug not substantially your own
- ❌ Submitting duplicates to same project for additional rewards
- ❌ Spam/low-quality reports, non-bug-report content
- ❌ Submitting in non-English
- ❌ No PoC or incomplete PoC when project requires it
- ❌ AI-generated/automated scanner reports lacking impact details
- ❌ Submitting fixes without project consent

### 4. Account Restrictions
- ❌ Creating multiple accounts
- ❌ Impersonation of other whitehats
- ❌ Failing to provide KYC in reasonable time → forfeiture
- ❌ Providing inauthentic KYC

### 5. Disclosure Restrictions
- ❌ Publicly disclosing bug reports before fixed+paid
- ❌ Failing to abide by Responsible Publication Policy
- ❌ Publicly posting screenshots from bug reports (except reward amount)
- ❌ Reporting already publicly disclosed bugs

### 6. Conduct Restrictions
- ❌ Requesting gas fees from Immunefi or projects
- ❌ Threats of violence or publishing personal info
- ❌ Extortion/blackmail
- ❌ Mediation request abuse

## Project Prohibited Behaviors

1. ❌ Mediation request abuse
2. ❌ "No fix, no pay" then stealth fixing later without full payment
3. ❌ Routing around Immunefi to communicate directly with whitehat
4. ❌ Claiming duplicate/known without clear evidence
5. ❌ Paying whitehats outside Immunefi
6. ❌ Publicly disclosing before fixed AND paid
7. ❌ Soliciting whitehats for commercial projects/private programs
8. ❌ Closing reports without detailed evidence
9. ❌ Refusing to provide invoicing info
10. ❌ Breaking SLAs

## Enforcement
- **Whitehats:** forfeiture + loss of access + zero payout
- **Projects:** removal + publication of removal for SLA breaks
- NO tolerance for spam, low-quality, beg bounty, misrepresentation

## SLA Timelines
- **48 hours** to acknowledge receipt (Critical)
- **96 hours** to acknowledge receipt (Non-critical)
- **21 days** for Critical/High resolution
- **14 days** for Low/Medium resolution
- If project breaks SLA → request Immunefi support in report

## Report Lifecycle
1. **Reported** → Submitted, not yet validated
2. **Escalated** → Passed Immunefi review, sent to project
3. **Confirmed** → Project believes valid, will pay
4. **Paid** → Payment sent, resolved
5. **Closed** → Project believes invalid/out of scope
   - If disagree: reply to thread or request mediation

## When to Escalate
- Wait up to 14 days for Critical
- After 14 days: poke project + Immunefi team
- Project has signed SLAs — hold them accountable
